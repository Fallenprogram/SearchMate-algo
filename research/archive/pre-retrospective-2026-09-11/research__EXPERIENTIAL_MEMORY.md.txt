# SearchMate experiential memory

Memory version: **m0-bootstrap**. Recorded: **2026-09-05**.

There are no accepted experimental lessons yet. Setup success, a plausible chess
idea, and an untested diagnosis are not validated reusable lessons. v0 construction
and calibration are setup evidence, not evidence that evolving memory improves
the Researcher.

Admit a lesson only after its supporting experiment completes and its evidence
checker accepts the claim. Use a separate memory-patch record and identify the
new memory version. Preserve superseded entries and append corrections.

Each accepted entry must contain:

- Stable lesson ID and concise lesson.
- Scope and preconditions for applying it.
- Producing experiment/run and exact supporting evidence.
- Relevant checker decision and alternative explanations.
- Known limitations and calibrated confidence.
- Active or superseded status, with replacement ID when applicable.

Failed hypotheses belong in the experiment history. They may support a narrowly
stated negative lesson only when the completed evidence warrants it. They are not
automatically accepted skills. Record the lesson IDs actually retrieved for each
future intervention rather than assuming that a saved lesson influenced it.
