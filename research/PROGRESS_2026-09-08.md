## Findings and limits

V7 produced a substantial local gain over v2 in its frozen comparison. The
combined change introduced a numeric board/search core, PVS and a score table;
no ablation isolated their contributions. It retains v2's evaluation and clock
formula, so remaining rated weaknesses cannot be considered resolved.

V8's extra time did not change any of six selected loss modes. These were late
development positions, not proof of the earliest losing choice or best defense.
The negative result does not establish an evaluation defect.

V9 reduced ordinary-plus-quiescence nodes from 67,416 to 51,572 over eleven
nonforced roots. Six roots saved at least 10%; one increased 1.63%. Each paired
root-depth score at depths one through three matched v7. In 36 native-clock API calls, ten modes
stayed unchanged and two control modes improved against saved bounded reference
estimates. Both changed modes were two-of-three, and baseline calls were reused
rather than newly interleaved. All six loss modes persisted. This is efficiency
and finite nonregression evidence, not a strength result.

The exact v9 ZIP initialized in 79.477 seconds and returned the expected legal
fallback on its first 1 ms request. Its 1.8969 ms round trip failed the frozen
check. That includes IPC, recording and scheduling overhead; it does not prove
an exception, weaker play or TT-specific causation. Seven package requests and
all games were unattempted. No retry or retrospective threshold change occurred.

A future protocol could separately assess practical fallback timing and
transport overhead before a strength screen. That proposal remains unexecuted;
this publication neither qualifies v9 nor starts another candidate.

## Recuris and preservation

The project adapts [Recuris](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24876) and its
[implementation](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/Recuris) as an external research
process: preserve observations, retrieve evidence, define isolated hypotheses,
bound checks, and retain failures and uncertainty. The engine uses CPU code,
with no game-time LLM or online weight update. Original SearchMate source is
separate from the offline reference engine used to estimate selected positions.

Historical memory remains **m0-bootstrap**, with no accepted experiential lesson
patch. Working records and checks evolved, but the deferred matched control
has not measured whether that evolution improves research efficiency or strength.
A successful player does not establish that causal benefit. Future lessons need
their producing experiment, scope, checker decision, alternatives and limits.

The original sealed final holdout remained unused. Opening block 3 was authorized
and exposed during v4; it cannot be presented as fresh in future work. Later
allocations retain their own exclusion history. The complete corpus is not
published here.

The historical local referee's prospective repetition-claim policy differs from
actual third-occurrence endpoints in several rated draws. Historical games retain
their original policy. A later external adapter was prepared but no v9 game used
it. The harness and all released player bytes remain unchanged.
